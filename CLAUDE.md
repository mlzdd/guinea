# Guinea Orchard

A cosy, office-friendly co-op farm for everyone on the LAN: up to 12 farmers share one farm. It starts small (the barn, the yard and 12 guinea pigs) and grows: buy squares of land in the shop, and the piggies breed. Feed them by throwing veg (they come running, wheeking), harvest and replant the veggie garden, gather apples in the orchard, fill the bowls in the barn, carry sacks from the feed bin to top up the pellet hopper, pick piggies up for a health check (weigh, nails, mites, sniffles, teeth: find problems and fix them), and shoo foxes and hawks before they carry anyone off. There's a barn (inside: walls drop and the roof comes off when you walk in) and the land around it, a 3×3 grid of squares (yard, veg patches, orchard, hay meadow, hut meadow, wild flowers, pond). Day turns to night; at dusk farmers serve the salad platter they've made up in the barn and everyone comes in for supper; at night the pigs sleep and foxes come more often. Each morning the farm gets a report card and coins for how the day went, to spend in the shop (B) on upgrades. **No sound effects**, just background music (day and night songs, `music.ts`; fine to play without, as office PCs often have no sound): pigs "say" everything in comic bubbles ("WHEEEK!", "monch monch", "zzz", "EEK!"). Sister project to `~/countersmack` (same toolchain and server pattern; that one is a brawler, this one is deliberately non-violent: carried-off pigs come back under the fence later, shaken).

Stack: Vite 8 + TypeScript 6 + three.js + `ws`, tested with Vitest 5, linted with oxlint. No React. All art is three.js primitives and canvas textures; no asset files.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `npm run dev` | Dev server + farm server on port 5173, reachable on the LAN |
| `npm run build && npm run preview` | Production build on port 4173 (farm server runs there too) |
| `npx vitest run` | Tests (`npm test` = watch mode) |
| `npm run restore` | List checkpoints; `npm run restore -- day003-1440` loads one (stop the server first) |
| `npx tsc -b` | Type-check app, server and tests |
| `npm run lint` | oxlint |

**Checkpoints:** every 10 minutes of farm-clock time (`EVERY_MIN` in server/checkpoints.ts) the server writes `data/checkpoints/dayNNN-HHMM.json` (the last farm day's worth, plus the 6am one of the last 14 days). `npm run restore` lists them, `npm run restore -- day003-1430` loads one (it refuses while a server is running on 5173/4173, since a server saves over the farm when it stops; the old save goes to `data/farm.before-restore.json`).

Find the LAN address with `hostname -I` (ignore `169.254.*`). If another computer can't connect: `sudo ufw allow 5173/tcp`. Delete `data/farm.json` for a fresh farm. Saves are versioned (`FarmSave.v`, now 2); a save of another version is ignored and the farm starts fresh.

## How it works

- **Server** (`server/plugin.ts`): a Vite plugin that attaches a WebSocket server at `/ws` to Vite's HTTP server (dev and preview); Vite's hot-reload socket is untouched. One farm per server. Ticks every 50 ms: `farm.tick(dt)`, sends queued messages, broadcasts a snapshot. The farm is saved to `data/farm.json` every 20 s while anyone's on, when the last farmer leaves, and on close; it's loaded on start (a broken save is ignored).
- **Farm clock:** `Farm.t` only advances while at least one farmer is on, so the pigs don't starve overnight. Days start short and get longer: day 1 is `FIRST_DAY_MS` (4 min), growing each day to `DAY_MS` (8 min) from day `DAY_RAMP` + 1 (`dayLength`). `farmDays(t)` turns farm time into days-with-fraction (`Farm.days`; `day` and `dayTime` come from it) and `farmTime(days)` goes back (handy in tests). Night is the last quarter of each day (`NIGHT_START`); once every pig is in the barn asleep for `SLEEP_SKIP_MS` with no predator about, the clock jumps to just before dawn (`skipNight`). `clockHours` turns time of day into the 6am–6am clock the HUD shows. Predators wait `FIRST_PREDATOR_MS` after the farm wakes.
- **Authority:** farmers own their own movement (sent 20×/s, pushed out of solids by the server). The server owns everything else: pigs, food, crops, bowls, baskets, predators. Interactions (`plant`, `harvest`, `fill`, `gather`, `pickup`, `treat`…) are requests the server checks (distance, basket room, state).
- **Pig brain** (`Farm.updatePig` / `decide`): hunger falls over time (slower asleep); grazing outside slows it. Danger first (flee to the nearest hidey hut or the barn; a hut's roofed sides and back are pig solids, so pigs go round and in or out only by its open front: `hutStep`, applied to every leg in `moveTo`), then begging near a farmer with veg, then the current state. Food landing excites every peckish pig within `EXCITE_RADIUS` → `seek` (client shows WHEEK) → `eat` (bites every `BITE_MS`, client shows monch). Bowls are foods with ids 0–3 that never go away. Sneaky pigs (`isSneaky`, `SNEAKY` of them, nearly half) often sneak into a veg patch by its gate when not full, even setting off from the barn if the door's open (`RAID_CHANCE`, `RAID_RANGE`, `raidTarget`/`startRaid`/`raiding`/`leaveRaid`, state `'raid'`, moving with `settleRaider` which ignores the gardens, routed round the barn, trees, huts and hay field and in by the gate (`pigPath` with `raid`: the garden fences are solid bar the gate gap; no way there, no raid). Any pig inside a garden (`Farm.inFence`: raiding, or left there) is held in by the fence and walks out by the gate (`Garden.gate`/`out`; `leaveRaid` heads there); the "sneaked in" alert comes when it gets there): munching fills them up but pauses a growing bed, or eats a ripe one's harvest a veg every `RAID_EAT_MS` (`Bed.eaten`, never the last one; `left` in the snapshot thins the bed out), and they stay put even when full (greedy), leaving only after `RAID_MS`, at dusk or in rain, or when a farmer comes within `RAID_SPOOK` (herding range) (a "Caught!" alert). Best friends seek each other out (`FRIEND_SEEK`) and stay side by side (`FRIEND_STAY`). They love snacks: below `SNACKY` they trot to veg/apples on the ground, and by day `SNACK_TRIP` sends them under an apple tree (graze) or to the garden fence by a ripe bed (beg at it; `snackSpot` / `arrived`). Poorly pigs (`poorly()`: hunger or happiness under `POORLY_HUNGER`/`POORLY_HAPPY`, or any health issue, hidden ones too) move at `POORLY_PACE`, skip popcorns, zoomies and snack trips, and `mope` (head down, sighing) in barn corners or along the fence (`mopeSpot`); the client draws them droopy, so you can spot who needs a check, food or a cuddle. Herding: a farmer moving at `HERD_SPEED`+ towards a calm pig (`HERDABLE` states) within `HERD_RADIUS` sends it scooting (`scoot`, `PIG_SCOOT`) `HERD_STEP` away and along their heading (`Farm.herd`, farmer speed from `Farmer.vx/vz` each tick); standing still doesn't, so you can still walk up and pick one up. At night pigs sleep at their own `BED_SPOTS` round the edges of the barn (little houses, corners, side walls, hay racks; `bedSpot`, stepping round them by `BED_STEP`, which shares no factor with how many there are, so each pig gets its own; `goodBed`); only `FOOD_SLEEPERS` may doze off on a food pile, nobody in the middle. A crowd doesn't pace: a pig heading to bed that comes up against one asleep (`SNUGGLE`) within `SNUGGLE_RANGE` of a bed spot curls up there (`snuggled`), and one that still can't get to bed after `BED_TRIES` goes (`Pig.bedTries`, reset by day) lies down where it is in the barn. Pigs path between inside and outside only through the barn door (`nextStep` in map.ts, with per-pig lanes so they don't jam), and round anything else in the way (barn walls, garden fences, trees, pond, hay field, huts): when the straight line is blocked (`pigLineBlocked`), `Farm.route` follows a `pigPath` (A* on a 0.5 m grid of where a pig fits, cached per land layout, straightened; at most every `PATH_EVERY_MS` per pig; train followers just follow). Raiders (and any pig inside a garden) use the same, with the garden fences solid bar their gates. Anything stuck for 1.5 s rethinks. Pigs overlapping get pushed apart (eating/sleeping/hiding ones stay put). Guinea pig trains: a pig on the move follows another just ahead going its way (`Farm.leader`, `Pig.follow`, `TRAIN_RADIUS`/`TRAIN_GAP`), `TRAIN_GAP` behind, until it's within `TRAIN_LEAVE` of its own spot or the leader stops.
- **Playtime:** in the hut meadow, a pig with a nearly full tummy and decent happiness (`PLAY_FULL`, `PLAY_HAPPY`, `PLAY_CHANCE`, `PLAY_RANGE`; by day, no rain) goes to play (`startPlay`/`playing`): state `'tunnel'` trots to a mouth of one of the `TUNNELS` (`tunnelMouth`) and scurries straight through (moveTo `straight`, no route-finding: the tube's sides are pig solids, the ends open) for `TUNNEL_HAPPY`, sometimes back again or a popcorn; state `'peek'` hides in a hidey hut for fun (`PEEK_MS`, `PEEK_HAPPY` a second; counts as hidden). Tunnels are low farmer blocks you can hop over. `Pig.play` is the tunnel×2+end (or the hut), `Pig.playing` once it's there.
- **Health:** problems are bit flags (`ISSUE_BIT`) that start at random (`ISSUE_RATE`; sniffles only from being outside at night). Mites make pigs scratch, sniffles make them sneeze; nails and teeth only show up in a health check. The check card (`hud.ts`) reveals each result after a short "checking…", then offers the fix.
- **Vet clinic** (`clinic` upgrade, barn tab; not the older `vet` upgrade, which only makes problems rarer): a pharmacy table along the right-hand wall of the barn, between the front right pig house and the second hopper (`VET_TABLE`, facing into the barn: `VET_FACE`; solid always as `VET_BLOCK`; before it's bought it's an empty table with a sign; the barn's second right-hand rack went to the inside of the front wall to make room). Holding a piggy near it, `vet` leaves it in `Farm.vetQueue` (up to `VET_QUEUE`; pig state `'vet'`, in `AWAY`): the first sits on the table (`VET_SLOTS[0]`, drawn `VET_TOP` up), the others in crates in front of it. Every `VET_MS` the vet (`updateVet`) fixes everything wrong with the one on the table (counted as treats, `fix` credited to whoever brought it), then lets it go at `VET_OUT`. The vet is a `FarmerModel` in the `'vet'` outfit standing at `World.vetStand`.
- **Gardener** (`gardener` upgrade, garden tab): a helper the server runs (`Farm.gardener`, a `Farmer` that isn't in `Farm.farmers`, so it doesn't count as someone on, or for pausing, the show or herding). `updateGardener` picks a job (`gardenerTask`: a sack for a hopper below `GARDENER_HOPPER`, then by day the nearest ripe bed, empty bed to replant, fallen apple or grown hay patch; once its basket has under `GARDENER_ROOM` left (`unloading`) or there's nothing to pick, it puts everything away: hay into racks with room, or haystacks, veg into low bowls first, then any bowl or the salad), walks there at `GARDENER_SPEED` along a `pigPath` (raider paths, so in by the garden gates; straight into the hay field from whichever edge works, `gardenerPath`), then does it with the same checked actions farmers use (`Farm.act`; `handle` is the farmer's way in), resting `GARDENER_WORK_MS`. A job that didn't change anything is skipped for 30 s. With nothing to do it stands at `GARDENER_HOME`. It works `GARDENER_ON` to `GARDENER_OFF` (7am to 7pm on `clockHours`; `gardenerOff`): at 7pm a sack in hand goes back in the bin and it walks out over the nearest non-barn fence (`fenceGate`, task `leave`), then it's `away` (not in the snapshot) till 7am, when it comes back in the same way (keeping its basket). The client walks its model after the server's position at its own pace, with a fixed pottering stride (`FarmerModel` `potter`), so it doesn't glide. `credit` ignores `GARDENER_NAME` (it can't be a player's name, as names can't have emoji). Sent as `gardener` in the snapshot; the client draws it in the `'gardener'` outfit (green dungarees, floppy sun hat, beard, green name tag).
- **Predators:** foxes sneak in from the west/east/south fence (slowly under the fence), go for the nearest exposed pig (outside, not in a hidey hut), pounce in the last few metres, then carry it slowly back under the fence. Pigs only notice a fox up close (closer still when eating). Hawks circle for a while (scaring pigs within `HAWK_FEAR`), then swoop. `F` shoos anything within `SHOO_RADIUS` (hawks a bit further) and makes it drop its pig. A pig that's carried off turns up at the south gate `LOST_MS` later, unhappy.
- **Pellets and hay:** hoppers are fixed food sources like the bowls (food ids `HOPPER_ID`+i, kind `'pellets'`). `sack` at the feed bin picks up/puts back a sack (hands full: no throwing or pig pickups); `pour` adds `sackPellets` (`SACK_PELLETS`, more with bigger hoppers). The second hopper only works once bought. Pellets are rationed: `Farm.sacks` is one per built hopper, restocked at dawn; when it's 0 you just can't take one (the prompt explains). Hay is the main food, from day one: the hay table (`HAY_BALE`, next to the feed bin) has a bale of `BALE_ARMFULS` armfuls (`Farm.bale`, restocked at dawn; `bale` message takes as many as fit in your basket). With the hay meadow there's more: `hay` on one of its `HAY_PATCHES` (a 4×4 field of tall hay; `hayPatchAt`) cuts an armful into your basket (`Farmer.hay` counts armfuls, each taking `HAY_SLOTS` places: `basketCount`; hands stay free) and the patch regrows over `HAY_REGROW_MS` (`Farm.hayField`, sent as `hayField` 0..1 and drawn as a block of standing hay that grows back); The field (`HAY_FIELD`) is solid to pigs, who'd be lost in it. The stack yard down the right of the field, nearest the barn (`HAY_STACKS`, `stack` message): tip all your hay onto a haystack (up to `HAY_STACK_MAX`) or, with none on you, take as much as fits, so hay can be brought in while the field's ready and fed to the racks later, `rack` puts as many armfuls (`HAY_ARMFUL` each) as fit into one of the `HAY_RACKS` (fixed foods from `RACK_ID`, kind `'hay'`, eaten from the floor in front: `rackFront`, by each rack's `face`). Racks are round the barn walls and outside its front; some only go up with the barn improvements (`HayRack.barn`, `rackBuilt`), and you can't fill one through the wall. New racks go on the end of the list (saves keep them by index). A pig that ate hay in the last day (`hayAt`) rarely gets bad teeth (`HAY_TEETH`); the report pays `PAY.hay` per pig that had hay.
- **Money:** one shared wallet (`Farm.coins`, starts at `START_COINS`). `payDay()` runs at dawn: happiness, well-fed pigs, fixes, rescues, minus carried-off pigs, pigs still out an hour after dark (`OUT_LATE`; calm pigs head in at dusk, `HEAD_HOME`, and sniffles only start after `OUT_LATE` too) and pigs still visibly poorly (`VISIBLE_ISSUES`: mites, sniffles; hidden nails and teeth only cost happiness) (`PAY`), sent as a `report` (with 1–5 stars, `STARS`). `buy` spends it on `UPGRADES`, a level at a time (`levels`: cost and desc each; a level is one more copy of the id in `Farm.upgrades`, so `level(u, id)` counts them and `nextLevel` is what's for sale; saves keep the copies, capped at the levels there are). Every effect is a helper by level in rules.ts next to the rule it changes (`basketMax`, `hopperMax`, `harvestYield`, `growMs`, `herdMax`, `appleEvery`, `hawkEvery`, `foxEvery`/`foxDig`, `cosy`, `issueRate`, `pregnancyRate`, `zoomFillMs`, `shooRadius`, `farmerSpeed`, `extraSacks`, `hayRegrowMs`) and `World.setUpgrades` shows the ones you can see (second hopper, scarecrow, sprinklers, heater, compost heap, wire on the fence, second veggie patch). The second veggie patch (`GARDEN2`, NE corner) is always fenced and solid; its beds (`BedDef.garden === 1`) only work once bought (`Farm.bedOpen`); before that it shows weeds and a for-sale sign. Coins, upgrades and hopper levels are saved (older saves load with the defaults). Farmers' baskets (veg and hay) are kept by name: `Farm.baskets` holds those of farmers who've gone home, `join` gives it back, and saves include everyone's (a sack in hand on leaving goes back in the bin).
- **Land** (map.ts): `SQUARES` is the 3×3 grid (`GRID`), `LAND` in rules.ts has each square's name, cost and description. `Farm.land` is what's owned (starts `START_LAND`: barn + yard); `land` message buys a square that `canBuy` (next to owned land). The map module holds the current land (`setLand`; the farm calls it on every tick/message as there's one map and tests make many farms, the client calls it from snapshots) and refills `FENCE_EDGES`/`FENCE`, `PIG_SOLIDS`, `FOX_SOLIDS`, `FARMER_BLOCKS` from it: only owned squares' things (garden fences, trees, huts, the hay field for pigs, pond) are solid, and the fence runs round the owned squares. Pigs and food stay on owned land (`onFarm`, `settlePig` pulls things back on, `pigCanStand`); farmers can roam anywhere (hop the fence). Foxes spawn outside a non-barn fence edge, exit straight out through the nearest edge that isn't blocked (`exitFrom`, `crosses`), and are slow near the fence (`atFence`). Square effects: hay meadow grazing fills tummies faster (`LUSH_GRAZE`), wild flowers cheer grazing pigs (`FLOWER_HAPPY`), resting by the pond too (`POND_HAPPY`). Things only exist once their square is owned: beds (`bedOpen`), `hideys()`, `trees()` and apples, `gardens()`; jobs and upgrades can `needs` a square. World.setLand shows owned squares' things and puts long grass and a for-sale sign on the rest; the shop has tabs (`SHOP_TABS`), Land first with a little map.
- **Land improvements:** every square (including barn and yard) has three cumulative tiers in the Land shop (`LAND_UPGRADES`, `land_*` upgrade ids). Each tile previews the next benefit and has an expandable roadmap. Effects stack with equipment upgrades: deeper racks, a pantry and snug bedding (barn); clover, play arches and a shooing bell (yard); yield/growth/replanting (both vegetable plots); double hay cutting, larger stacks and faster regrowth (meadow); extra fallen-fruit capacity, freshness and double drops (orchard); comfort, weather protection and earlier fox warnings (huts); grazing, happiness and a crop growth bonus (flowers); wider calm, happiness and fewer health problems (pond). Rules and `LAND_BONUS` live in rules.ts; `World.buildLandImprovements` draws cumulative decorations. Purchases rescale crops already growing, saves retain tier counts, and resource prompts/rendering use the upgraded capacities. Regression coverage: tests/core/land-upgrades.test.ts.
- **Breeding** (`Farm.breed`, each tick): a happy, fed adult sow, with an adult boar on the farm and room for the litter, conceives at `PREGNANCY_PER_DAY` (× `pregnancyRate`, the nursery). `due` (on the look, in farm days: `Farm.days`) is `PREGNANCY_DAYS` later; while expecting she gets hungry faster (`PREGNANT_HUNGER`) and `careGood`/`careTotal` track how long she's been kept at `CARE_OK`+ hunger and happiness (cuddles count `CARE_CUDDLE_S`; shown as `care` on the snapshot and the check card). `giveBirth`: 2–5 pups by `LITTER` (from `LITTER_MIN`; 5 is rare), at most 3 if care < 50%, 2 if < 25%, capped by `herdMax`. Pups (`age` 0, `born` in farm days) are drawn growing from half size, always stay by mum, and grow up after `PUP_DAYS`.
- **Salad night:** farmers put veg from their basket into the salad at `SALAD_TABLE` (`salad` message, up to `saladMax(pigs)`: `SALAD_PER_PIG` veg per piggy, at least `SALAD_MAX`, counting platters being carried); it's ready at `SALAD_MIN` veg of `SALAD_KINDS`+ kinds and can be served from `SALAD_FROM` (just before nightfall), once a night (no more veg goes in once the first platter's down). A platter holds up to `PLATTER_MAX`, so a big herd needs `platters(pigs)` of them, each with its own spot on the barn floor (`SALAD_SPOTS`, enough for the biggest barn): `serve` at the station picks one up (`dishUp` takes a veg of each kind in turn into `Farmer.platter`, hands full: `handsFull`) while there's veg left and a free spot nobody's carrying one to (`canServe`, `freeSpots`); `serve` within `PLATTER_PLACE` of a free spot puts it down (the client shows glowing rings on the free spots while one's carried, `Game.freePlatterSpots`; `serve` at the station puts it back, veg and all: `platterBack`, also on leaving and at dawn). While one's carried every calm peckish pig within `PLATTER_LURE` goes `'chase'`: wheeking along close behind the nearest carrier (`platterCarrier`), fanned out. Served, each is a fixed food (`SALAD_ID` + spot) with `SALAD_BITES` per veg; every pig that isn't full is excited towards it (they come in through the door), each pig sticking to one of the platters out (by id, so they spread round), and the rest go home. Each bite gives 2 + the number of kinds in happiness; the report pays `PAY.salad` + `PAY.saladKind` per kind. There's a dusk reminder; leftovers are cleared at dawn.
- **Pigs as people:** `PigLook` also carries the things that change rarely: `name` (renamed from the check card), `adopter` (farmer name, ⭐), `friend` (pairs `id ^ 1`; they hang out, a pig is sadder while its friend is carried off and cheers when it comes back), `mum`/`born` and `due` (see Breeding) and `rosettes`. Any change is broadcast as a `pig` message (also how clients hear of a birth); `welcome` sends them all. Pig ids are indexes into `Farm.pigs`, which can grow (up to `herdMax`).
- **Each dawn** (`payDay` → `newDay`): pay (with the craving line), then a new `craving` (a bite of it gives `CRAVING_HAPPY`), `JOBS_PER_DAY` new jobs, maybe a rain shower (`rainFrom`/`rainTo`), and the barn door opens. A fresh farm's craving and jobs are picked without the dice so seeded tests don't shift.
- **Pig show** (show.ts for the layout, rivals, judging and timings; `Farm.updateShow` and friends): every `SHOW_EVERY` days at `SHOW_AT` the show car parks at `CAR_FARM` (phase `'boarding'`, `SHOW_BOARD_MS`). `board` at the car toggles `Farmer.aboard` (with the piggy you're holding, or to watch; the client keeps you still and unseen). When time's up or every farmer's in, `departShow`: aboard farmers become `atShow` entrants with spots (`SPOTS`) and are `teleport`ed (a message: the client moves itself) to the show barn at `HALL` (x≈200, far enough that the fog hides it; `settleFarmer` uses `HALL_BLOCKS`/`SHOW_AREA` there). `judge` puts your piggy on the table (pig state `'show'`, in `AWAY`), the judge scores it after `JUDGE_MS` (`judge()`: tummy, happy, health, handling, condition, 20 each), `fetch` picks it back up, and you're `back` once you hold it at your spot. When the last entrant with a piggy is back (or `SHOW_MS` runs out) `showResults`: everyone placed against the rivals' `rivalScores` (`RIVALS`: the same breeders and piggies every show, `Farm.rivalSkill` drifting each time), `SHOW_PRIZES` by place (100/50/20/10, rivals count) and `SHOW_TAKING_PART` for every other farm piggy judged (a rosette for a winner), `showBoard` (all-time wins/podiums/best, saved with `rivalSkill` and `showDay`), a `showResults` message. After `RESULTS_MS` `endShow` teleports everyone home with their piggies. Nobody boards: it goes on without the farm. Leaving mid-show sends your piggy home (`leaveShow`). Client: `ShowHall` (showhall.ts: the barn, judge, rivals holding their piggies with their rosettes, onlookers, spot names, the car), `Game.showAction` prompts, `Hud.showJudging` and `showShowResults`. Where to go next (`ShowGo`: the table to be judged or to fetch your piggy, then your spot) gets a glowing ring and sign, a gold chevron on the ground at your feet pointing the way, and a gold edge-of-screen arrow (🏆/📍) when it's off screen.
- **Jobs and the diary:** `Farm.credit(name, stat)` is called wherever a farmer does something (`STATS`); it counts towards today's jobs (paid `JOB_PAY` the moment one is done) and the all-time `diary` (by farmer name, saved). The diary is sent on request (`diary` message, client asks every 3 s while it's open with `L`). Food remembers who put it out (`Food.by`) so wheeks (pigs that came running) are credited.
- **Barn door:** `doorShut` (`door` message near `DOOR_MID`). Shut: pigs are pushed out of `DOOR_GATE`, don't cross to food or wander across, and shut-out pigs queue at the door begging (`waitAtDoor`). Farmers step over it. Foxes can only get into the barn at night with the door open (`barnOpen`: inside pigs then count as `exposed` to foxes, foxes route with `nextStep` via `foxStep`, and can always squeeze back out).
- **Zoomies:** the zoomometer (`zoomMeter`, 0..1, in the snapshot and under the clock) charges while the herd's average happiness is at least `ZOOM_HAPPY` (in `ZOOM_FILL_MS`, faster when happier), drains below it and rests at night. When full: `ZOOMIES_PAY` coins and `ZOOMIES_MS` of happy pigs alternating popcorns and short dashes (`zoom` state), then it starts again from empty. **Rain**: calm pigs go in (`goIn`), wanders stay inside, sniffles can start outside, growing beds go `RAIN_GROW`× faster; the client greys the sky and draws rain and puddles (`World.update`).
- **Pause:** `P` sends `pause`, which toggles `Farm.paused` (who paused it, in the snapshot). While paused `tick` does nothing (no clock, pigs, crops or predators) and `handle` ignores everything but `pause` and `diary`; the client freezes its farmer and all animation and shows the `#paused` banner. Anyone can carry on; when the last farmer leaves it unpauses. Not saved.
- **Emotes and jumping:** Z X C V send `emote` (`EMOTES`), broadcast to everyone as a bubble over the farmer. Movement has momentum (`moveFarmer` in move.ts, run by the client: `FARMER_ACCEL`/`FARMER_STOP` on the ground, only `AIR_ACCEL` of steering in the air, walls take away speed). Space jumps for real (`JUMP_V`/`GRAVITY`), keeping your speed: farmers have a feet height `y` (sent in `state`, shown in the snapshot). Every farmer obstacle is a `Block` with a height (`FARMER_BLOCKS`): `settleFarmer(p, y)` only pushes you out of things taller than your feet, and `groundAt` is what you land on. Garden fences (0.75), the farm fence (1.2), hidey huts (`HIDEY_H`) can be jumped onto or over; barn walls and trunks can't. Farmers can roam all the land and `FARMER_ROAM` beyond it. On a desktop a mouse click only throws (E does what the prompt says); on touch a tap throws and the ✋ button acts. Clicking a basket slot (`Hud.onSlot`) holds that veg, or the hay; clicking it again drops one at your feet (a `throw` just in front of you). Hay can be thrown too: an armful lands as a ground pile of `HAY_ARMFUL` bites.
- **Touch screens** (`device.ts`: `TOUCH` from `pointer: coarse`, or `?touch=1`/`?touch=0`; `body.touch`): `Input` has a stick that stays put in the bottom left (`#stick`, placed by CSS); a thumb on it, within `STICK_GRAB` of its middle or in the corner past it pushes it (pushed right out = run, `STICK_RUN`), a tap elsewhere into a throw (`onTap`; holding a piggy with its check card up, a tap places it down; stray taps with hands full or nothing to throw do nothing), a hold into the hover label (`HOLD_MS`) and two fingers into zoom. `TouchControls` (touch.ts) adds buttons that `press` keys: ✋ action (lit when there's something to do; the prompt can be tapped too), 📣 shoo, ⤴️ jump, 😀 emotes, and 🛒 📖 ❓ ⏸️ down the top right, 📋 (farmers and jobs drawer) top left. Key hints are `.keys`, tap hints `.taps` (CSS shows one or the other; `kbd` is hidden on touch). The phone layout is the `body.touch` and `max-height: 500px` rules at the end of style.css (the check card goes two columns, the end-of-day report packs onto fewer lines); the check card covers the farm on a phone, so it has a Hide button and walking with a piggy tucks it away (`Hud.foldCheck`, `checkOpen`, `#check-tab` brings it back), so you can carry a piggy to the show car or judging table; phones start zoomed in (`camDist`); a portrait phone gets "turn sideways" (`#rotate`), joining goes full screen and tries to lock landscape (switching away drops it; the first tap back in the game puts it back, as browsers only allow it from a tap). **Graphics**: `FAST` (default on touch; Nice/Fast buttons in the help, saved as `guinea.quality`, `?quality=fast`) means no antialiasing, pixel ratio ≤ 1.5, a 1024 shadow map, two fur shells and fewer hairs (`hair()` in pig.ts).
- **Client** has no pointer lock: the mouse aims at the ground (throws land there, capped at `THROW_RANGE`), the camera stays at its starting angle, the wheel zooms. Bubbles are client-side, driven by pig state changes plus periodic chatter (`Game.chatter`), only for pigs within `CHATTER_RANGE` of you.
