  • Hungry piggies come running when veg lands near them.
  • The garden 🥕 regrows after you replant it. Apples 🍎 fall in the orchard.
  • Hay is their main food: once you have the 🌾 hay meadow, cut armfuls in the field, build haystacks in the stack yard beside it, and carry hay to the racks in the barn. Pellets are rationed: one sack per hopper a day, from the feed bin by the door.
  • Each morning the farm is paid for how happy the piggies are. Spend it in the shop (B).
  • Pick piggies up for a health check: nails, mites, sniffles, teeth. Adopt and rename your favourites there too.
  • A slow, mopey piggy sitting in a corner is hungry, glum or unwell: feed it, cuddle it or give it a check.
  • Walk at piggies to herd them along (into the barn at dusk, say). Stand still and they stay put.
  • The piggies crave a different veg each day 🤤, and there are jobs to do 📋 for bonus coins.
  • Make up a salad platter at the salad station in the barn (10+ veg, 3+ kinds) and serve it at dusk: everyone comes in for supper 🥗.
  • At night, get everyone indoors and shut the barn door: foxes come out.
  • Buy more land in the shop 🗺️: a square at a time, next to land you have.
