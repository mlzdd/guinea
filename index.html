  • 🥕 Throw veg and hungry piggies come running. Replant beds after harvest; 🍎 apples fall in the orchard.
  • 🌾 Hay is their main food: take it off the bale by the barn door (new every morning) to the racks. The hay meadow grows more.
  • 🥣 Pellets are rationed: a sack per hopper a day, from the feed bin.
  • 🩺 Pick a piggy up to check nails, mites, sniffles, teeth. Adopt and rename there too.
  • 😔 Slow and mopey = hungry, glum or poorly: feed, cuddle or check it.
  • 🐹 Walk at piggies to herd them; stand still and they stay.
  • 🤫 Sneaky piggies raid the veg beds: walk over to catch them.
  • 🥗 Dusk: serve the salad platter (10+ veg, 3+ kinds) and they all come in.
  • 🌙 Night: everyone in, shut the barn door. Foxes!
  • 🏆 Show day: carry your best-kept piggy to the show car, put it on the judging table, then back to your spot for the results.
  • 🪙 Happy piggies earn coins each morning. 🤤 Cravings and 📋 jobs pay extra. Spend it on upgrades and 🗺️ land (B).
